Brandon Salerno (Host) is a NYC-based actor with a passion for Clown, Commedia dell’Arte, and Farce. Best known for his recurring role as Truman in The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel (Seasons 1, 2 & 3). Additional credits: The Other Two (HBO Max), Nella The Princess Knight (Nick Jr.), and Who, When, Wow! (Apple Podcasts). Founding member of The Department of Fools. Reps: Trillion Management, Stewart Talent, and BMG.
